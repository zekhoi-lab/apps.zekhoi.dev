Labs is a collection of developer tools made by zekhoi, an independent developer. It has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics, and it does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal details.

## What you type into the tools

The tools do their work in your browser. What you paste or type into them is not sent to a server.

The HTTP Client is the one exception, because sending requests is its job. It sends the request you write from your browser to the address you enter.

## What Labs stores

Labs keeps these in your browser's local storage, on the device you use:

- your theme choice
- your recent UUIDs, in the UUID Generator
- your draft, in the Markdown Editor

This data stays in your browser.

## Other services

Labs is hosted on Vercel. When you open it, Vercel receives the technical data any website receives: your IP address, browser user agent, the files requested, and the time. This data is used to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, as described in the [Vercel privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

Labs loads its icon font from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google when a page opens.

## Removing your data

Clear the site data for labs.zekhoi.dev in your browser to remove everything Labs stored.
