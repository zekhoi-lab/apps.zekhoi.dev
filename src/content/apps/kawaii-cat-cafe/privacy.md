Kawaii Cat Cafe is a browser game made by zekhoi, an independent developer. It has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal details.

## What the game stores

The game keeps these in your browser's local storage, on the device you play on:

- your cafe save
- your settings, such as music and sound volume
- which tips you have already seen
- when you last played, so the cafe can catch up on the days you were away

This data stays in your browser. The game does not send it to a server.

## Save files

In Settings you can save your cafe as a file and load it again. The file is written to your device and goes nowhere else unless you share it yourself.

## Other services

The game loads its font from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google when the game opens.

## Removing your data

Choose "start a new cafe" in Settings to replace your save, or clear the site data for kawaii.zekhoi.dev in your browser to remove everything the game stored.

Email [me@zekhoi.dev](mailto:me@zekhoi.dev) with any question about this policy.
