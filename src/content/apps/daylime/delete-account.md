You can delete your Daylime account at any time, from inside the app or by email if you can no longer open it. This permanently deletes your account, check-ins, and conversations.

## Using Daylime without an account

Without an account, your check-ins are only on your phone. Go to Settings, then Your data, then Delete all data on this phone to remove everything.

## Before you delete

Deleted check-ins and conversations cannot be recovered. Note down anything you want to keep first.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open Daylime and sign in.
2. Go to Settings, then Account & data.
3. Tap Delete account.
4. Confirm.

For your security, deleting works within a day of signing in. If the app asks, sign in again first.

## Delete your account without the app

If you have uninstalled Daylime or cannot sign in, email us to request deletion.

1. Send an email to [me@zekhoi.dev](mailto:me@zekhoi.dev?subject=Delete%20my%20Daylime%20account) from the address on your Daylime account, with the subject "Delete my Daylime account".
2. We verify that the request comes from you.
3. We delete the account and all associated data.

We complete deletion requests within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

## What gets deleted

- Your account, email address, and display name
- Your check-ins, with their moods, notes, and activity tags
- Your conversations with Limi, including chat summaries
- Your choices about what Limi may see

App settings such as theme and reminder time are stored only on your phone, not in your account.

## What we keep, and for how long

- Database restore points can hold deleted data for up to 7 days. They are then deleted.
- If the law requires us to keep a record, we keep only that record, and only for as long as required.

## After deletion

Deletion cannot be undone. If you want to use Daylime again, you can create a new account, but your old check-ins will not come back.

For anything else about your data, see the [privacy policy](/daylime/privacy) or email [me@zekhoi.dev](mailto:me@zekhoi.dev).
