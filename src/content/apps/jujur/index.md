Jujur means honest in Indonesian. The review site at jujur.review names the strengths and the weaknesses of each product and says whether it is worth buying.

Jujur Studio is where those reviews are made. Creators keep their products, affiliate links, and characters in a workspace, write articles with help from AI, and submit them for checking.

Jujur is in early access, so things may change between visits.
