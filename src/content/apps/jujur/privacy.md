This policy covers the Jujur review site at jujur.review and Jujur Studio at studio.jujur.review. Jujur is made by zekhoi, an independent developer.

For anything about this policy or your data, email [me@zekhoi.dev](mailto:me@zekhoi.dev).

## Reading the review site

You can read jujur.review without an account. It has no ads and no analytics scripts.

When you click a buy link, Jujur records the click before sending you to the shop. The record holds the link, the article you came from, your country, the page that referred you, and scrambled forms of your IP address and browser user agent. The scrambled forms are one-way hashes, so they let us count visitors without storing the address itself. Creators see these clicks as totals in the studio.

## Using Jujur Studio

| Data           | What it includes                                                                                                       |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Account data   | Your name, email address, and password. The password is stored as a hash, not as written.                              |
| Workspace data | Your workspaces, their members, and the invitations you send.                                                          |
| Your content   | Products, affiliate links, characters, articles, and the photos and videos you upload or generate.                     |
| AI usage       | The requests you send to AI tools, their results, and their cost. If you save your own AI key, it is stored encrypted. |
| Sign-in        | A cookie that keeps you signed in.                                                                                     |

You sign in with your email address and a password. We email you a link to confirm your address, and another if you reset your password.

## How we use it

- To run the studio: store your workspace, generate content, and publish approved articles.
- To check articles: each submitted article goes through an AI check and then an editor.
- To show creators how their links perform.
- To contact you about your account, for example with a confirmation link.

We do not sell your data.

## Who we share it with

| Provider    | What it does                                                     |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cloudflare  | Hosts both sites and stores uploaded and generated files         |
| Supabase    | Hosts the database, in Seoul, South Korea                        |
| OpenRouter  | Routes AI requests to the AI model that writes, draws, or checks |
| Trigger.dev | Runs the background jobs that generate and check content         |
| Resend      | Delivers account emails                                          |

When you use an AI tool, the product data, character details, and instructions for that request are sent to OpenRouter and the AI model it routes to.

Articles you publish are public, with the character shown as the author.

## Deleting your data

You can delete products, characters, articles, and files in the studio. To delete your whole account, see [Delete Account](/jujur/delete-account).
