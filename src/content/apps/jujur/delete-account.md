You can ask for your Jujur Studio account to be deleted at any time. Jujur Studio has no delete button yet, so deletion is by email.

## How to delete your account

1. Send an email to [me@zekhoi.dev](mailto:me@zekhoi.dev?subject=Delete%20my%20Jujur%20account) from the address on your Jujur account, with the subject "Delete my Jujur account".
2. We verify that the request comes from you.
3. We delete the account and the data listed below.

## What gets deleted

- Your account, name, email address, and password
- Workspaces that only you belong to, with their products, affiliate links, characters, articles, and files
- Your saved AI key, if you added one

If you share a workspace with other members, the workspace stays with them and you are removed from it.

## After deletion

Deletion cannot be undone. Articles from a deleted workspace are taken off the review site.

For anything else about your data, see the [privacy policy](/jujur/privacy) or email [me@zekhoi.dev](mailto:me@zekhoi.dev).
